Bundled MPV portable build
==========================

WARDOGS Radio includes the unmodified x64 portable MPV files from the
2026-09-24 build published by shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake:

  https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake/releases/tag/20260924
  mpv-x86_64-20260924-git-2a4eb8067c.7z
  SHA-256: 0d39c18086f9df02fbd087b18c8b30e7e328a79481bce83e57f86ab70d0e53bf

WARDOGS Radio starts MPV as a separate process and does not modify its files.
MPV source code and license information are available from:

  https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  https://github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake

The bundled files are replaced only by a future WARDOGS Radio installer; this
installer does not register a system-wide MPV installation or modify a user's
separately installed copy of MPV.
